## Agreed product

EcoVibes connects economic participation in Africa: work, services, commerce, creators, communities and circular-economy activity. One Eco ID, consistent trust, permissioned data, low-data interfaces and regulated payment providers connect the layers.

- **Quick&Handi / Eco Services:** partners list services; eligible providers quote; customers select, coordinate, confirm or dispute work.
- **Eco Market:** direct listings and CSV remain first-class. Store integrations use adapters; Shopify is one connector, not the catalog model.
- **Waste-to-Money:** a distinct business within EcoVibes. Collector-to-plant matching, material compatibility, price freshness, receiving status, weight evidence, disputes and verified settlement are required. General waste pickups belong to EcoVibes Collect.
- **Chale AI:** a permission-aware guide and tool interface. It uses actual app data and user-controlled learning. A deterministic recommendation algorithm must not be described as a model that trains itself. Actions involving money, account changes or other people require appropriate authorization and confirmation.
- **Shared identity:** personal accounts, organization workspaces and platform staff have different powers. An organization account cannot automatically see another organization's private data. An organization administrator is not a platform administrator.
- **Ghana first, configurable countries later:** enable countries and languages when their service, support, payment and regulatory dependencies are ready. Avoid hard-coded identity architecture.

The recovered discussion also names Passport, Business, Trade, Access/Lite, Skills, expanded Opportunities, Agro, Power and Health Workforce as future directions. Their complete original specifications were not recovered. They are not launch-complete modules.
