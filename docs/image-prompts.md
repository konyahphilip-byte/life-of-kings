# EcoVibes image prompts

Use these prompts with an image tool. Keep people and place photography natural and respectful. The current Home cards contain labeled procedural placeholders (`data-image-slot`) that can be replaced by cropped images when final assets are ready. Export WebP or AVIF, preserve the recommended crop, and keep the focal subject away from the text-safe area.

## 1. Hero banner — `hero-banner`

**Suggested crop:** 3:2 landscape, with a clean left-side text-safe area and visual focus on the right.

Create a premium editorial campaign image for EcoVibes, a community platform connecting African creators, local service professionals, gamers and entrepreneurs. Show a small, diverse group of contemporary Ghanaian adults in Accra at first light on a rooftop studio terrace: a woman filmmaker checking a compact cinema camera, a male product designer with a tablet, and a woman artisan carrying a sculptural woven lamp. Let the figures feel candid, self-assured and collaborative. Behind them, let the city skyline and distant coastal light fade into a soft savanna-sunrise gradient. Integrate delicate luminous paths and a few constellation points into the sky, plus a very faint woven geometric rhythm in the architecture. Warm natural dawn light, deep forest green and soft leaf green as the main palette, with restrained brass gold and terracotta accents. Sophisticated contemporary African art direction, realistic skin texture, natural proportions, quiet optimism, crisp high-end photography, airy composition, generous pale negative space on the left for dark headline copy. No text, logos, flags, costumes used as shorthand, poverty imagery, exoticizing props, or tourist-poster clichés.

## 2. Marketplace — `marketplace`

**Suggested crop:** 4:3 landscape or 1:1 card image; keep faces and products inside the central 70%.

An elevated editorial photograph of a contemporary Accra makers market, focused on two Ghanaian independent makers presenting small-batch ceramic tableware, hand-dyed fabric and a beautifully finished leather bag to a customer. The people are modern, diverse adults in their own everyday style, engaged in a real conversation and visibly proud of their work. Clean stalls, considered product display, leafy shade and a glimpse of city life. Add subtle translucent interface-like light trails behind the products, suggesting discovery and secure local commerce without any readable UI or text. Soft late-morning natural light, warm earth tones, brand forest greens, a little brass and terracotta, tactile materials, premium documentary photography. Keep the background calm and uncluttered, with no poverty cues, no staged “exotic market” styling, no logos and no text.

## 3. Gaming — `gaming`

**Suggested crop:** 4:3 landscape; leave one corner low-detail for the card title overlay.

Photograph a close-knit group of young adult African friends in a modern Accra gaming lounge, sharing a joyful but focused co-op match at a console and two desktop stations. Include a woman esports player in the foreground and friends of different genders and skin tones in the midground. Contemporary streetwear, quality equipment, warm practical lighting and a confident, locally grounded atmosphere. Reflect a few abstract green and gold game lights in the room; add a subtle dotted constellation motif across a dark wall as a visual echo of connection. Deep leafy green, charcoal, muted gold and a small electric-lime accent. Cinematic editorial photography, candid expressions, realistic hands and equipment, no legible game UI, no brand logos, no stereotypes and no neon overload.

## 4. Opportunities — `opportunities`

**Suggested crop:** 4:3 landscape; reserve the right third as lower-detail space for a text label.

An optimistic editorial photograph of a Ghanaian technology team reviewing a project at a bright coworking studio in Accra. A Black woman founder leads the conversation while a male software engineer and a woman industrial designer share notes on a large tablet. Their expressions are attentive and collaborative, not posed at the camera. Through a window, show a soft urban Accra skyline in clear morning light. Hint at opportunity and momentum with one fine glowing path connecting small points on a translucent glass board, no readable interface. Natural skin tones, warm sunlight, restrained forest green, pale sage, dark earth and a small brass accent. Credible contemporary workplace, premium magazine photography, realistic detail, no stock-photo handshakes, no logos or text, no poverty or deficit framing.

## 5. Quick&Handi — `quickhandi`

**Suggested crop:** 4:3 landscape; keep the artisan and their hands visible in the right half.

Show practical tools used safely, attentive listening and mutual respect; the professionals are skilled collaborators, not background labor. Include subtle clues to an urban Ghanaian home through plants, warm painted walls and patterned tile, without turning the setting into a decorative stereotype. Early-afternoon natural light, grounded leafy greens, cream, muted rust and a hint of brass. Contemporary and capable, realistic people and hands, premium photography, no logos, no text, no unsafe electrical work, no poverty imagery, no “savior” framing.
