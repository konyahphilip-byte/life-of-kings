# Marketplace 5.0 — 5 November 2026

The founder's 1762-line Marketplace 5.0 brief is the current development focus. Build on existing EcoVibes; native selling is the default and must not require Shopify, subscriptions or technical store ownership.

First establish genuine seller/product/category/photo/publication/search/inventory journeys; then complete cart/order/payment/fulfillment acceptance, African seller tools and approved Chale/Request Market integration. Optional connectors, suppliers, rentals, group buying and other pilots follow actual dependencies and demand. Do not build every connector or speculative table.

Preserve legitimate and uncertain records. Separate known device examples from current seller stock; never invent stock, prices, verification, reviews, payments or tracking. Source identifiers, seller ownership, current inventory and independent role verification remain authoritative.

Core access/listing/verification/subscriptions/commissions remain GH₵0 at launch. Existing third-party charges must be disclosed accurately; no paid integration, resource or financial commitment is activated.

Each increment needs actual code, tests/retained failures, build/security checks, a reviewable commit/PR, current audit/readiness records and explicit local/CI/hosted/device/provider scope. No production deployment, destructive migration, real payment or external-store mutation without explicit approval.

The current native-catalog slice is a partial Milestone1 prerequisite. It does not complete seller verification, hierarchical categories/dynamic attributes, production photo storage, real storefront acceptance or a genuine settled transaction.
