Beta service terms

Updated 1 October 2026 · Initial service summary

EcoVibes is being tested in stages. The operator’s legal entity and final service terms still need owner review before a public launch. By using the beta, use only the features identified as available and report safety or transaction problems through Settings → Report a problem or contact support.

Accounts and content

  • Keep your sign-in details private and provide accurate listing, offer and account information.
  • Only upload or import product information and media that you have permission to use.
  • Do not use EcoVibes to threaten, harass, defraud, expose another person’s private information, or publish unlawful or unsafe material. Use the in-app reporting flow to raise a concern.
  • Reports are placed in a staff review queue. Automated content moderation and self-service blocking controls are not yet available in this beta.

Marketplace and Quick&Handi

Listings, stock, order states, job offers and fulfillment status are recorded by the service. Buyers and sellers should confirm product details, delivery arrangements, job scope and any applicable provider terms before proceeding. A listing or provider profile is not a guarantee of availability, quality or completion. Report a cancellation, payment or service problem promptly through the support form.

Payments, Wallet and rewards

Paystack checkout and refunds in this beta are restricted to test mode. The Wallet display does not hold money, and sample balances, points and badges have no cash value. Do not use a test checkout as proof of a real purchase. EcoVibes does not provide bank accounts, stored-value accounts or cash transfers in this beta.

AI and recommendations

Recommendations are generated from eligible user-selected interests and product, job and creator-content activity. You can pause personalization or reset learned activity. Chale AI may be unavailable and currently searches supported public product and open-job listings; it does not place orders, change balances or approve staff actions. Review generated information before relying on it.

Preview features and changes

Some sections are previews backed by sample content or local browser storage rather than shared services. Integrations such as store imports, media, AI and payments work only when explicitly configured. EcoVibes may change or disable beta features while testing them. These initial terms are a product summary, not the final contract for a public launch.

Support

Use EcoVibes ID → Trust & inbox to report a safety concern, account issue, dispute or transaction problem. A report is placed in the support review queue; this beta does not promise a response time or resolution outcome.
