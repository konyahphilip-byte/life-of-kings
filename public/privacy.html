Privacy notice

Updated 1 October 2026 · Product privacy summary

This notice describes the current EcoVibes beta. The operator’s legal entity, final retention periods and contact details still need to be confirmed before a public launch. For an account or privacy request, use Settings → Report a problem or contact support and choose “Account.”

Information EcoVibes handles

  • EcoVibes ID and sign-in: your display name, EcoVibes ID, account roles, and the account data Supabase Auth uses to sign you in. EcoVibes receives a verified Supabase user identity and keeps a linked EcoVibes profile.
  • Marketplace and Quick&Handi: product listings, stock, order and fulfillment records, job requests, offers, job messages, and support or dispute reports needed to operate those services.
  • Stories, Reels and follows: content and media you submit, its visibility setting, follows, and interaction records such as views and likes. Follower-only Stories are shown only to the author and followers.
  • Device data: this beta stores some preferences, drafts and preview activity in your browser. They may remain on that device until you clear browser storage.
  • Payment and store providers: checkout and refunds are handled through the configured provider. EcoVibes records the order and provider references required to reconcile them. The current Paystack integration is test-mode only; the demo Wallet has no real funds.

Personalized discovery

If personalization is enabled, one interest profile for your EcoVibes ID can use your selected interests, product and job activity, confirmed product purchases, and eligible Story, Reel and creator-follow activity. Supported EcoVibes features share this topic profile to rank relevant listings, Reels and creator suggestions for that same account. This beta does not pool identifiable activity across different users. It stores topic signals rather than using private message content, passwords, payment credentials, precise location or account recovery codes for ranking. Signals expire after 365 days and older activity has less influence. You can pause new recommendation learning or reset its learned signals in the Home recommendations panel; resetting does not remove order, social or other service records.

Service providers and AI

Supabase provides authentication, database and configured media storage; Paystack handles the test payment flow; Shopify is used only when a seller connects a store. Optional AI and live-media features send the data needed for the requested feature to the provider configured by EcoVibes. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information in AI prompts. Provider availability and configuration determine which optional features work.

Cookies, access and requests

EcoVibes uses an HttpOnly session cookie for the API and a CSRF token for state-changing requests. Staff review tools require a staff role and, in production, a verified second factor. A user can sign out to revoke their EcoVibes sessions. To request access, correction or deletion, contact support from EcoVibes ID; self-service export and deletion are not yet available in this beta.

Beta limitations

Some screens contain local previews or sample content. They are labeled in the app and should not be treated as shared account records. The privacy notice must be updated when new data sources, payment providers, analytics or retention rules are introduced.
